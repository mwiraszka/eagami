Eagami collects the bare minimum needed to keep the site running and to know which pages people find useful. There is no advertising and nothing that follows you to other sites.

What the site collects

Traffic is measured with Plausible Analytics, which reports which pages were opened, which country a visit came from, and which site or search led there. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

The site is hosted by Vercel, one of the platforms most modern sites are deployed on, which keeps the usual server logs of the requests it serves.

Your theme and language preferences are saved by your browser so the site remembers them next time. They never leave your device and are cleared with your browser data.

When you get in touch

The contact form sends your name, email address, and message to Eagami as an email. It is used to reply to you and nothing else: it is not sent to any third party and is not added to any mailing list.

The component library

The @eagami/ui package does not collect anything. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, so installing it or building with it sends nothing back to Eagami.

Questions

If you have any questions about our privacy policy, please email privacy@eagami.com.
