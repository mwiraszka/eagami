The short version

eagami.com has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other sites. The only personal information it receives is what you type into the contact form.

Contact form

When you send a message, your name, email address, and the message itself are emailed to info@eagami.com. They are not written to a database and are not used for marketing. The message is kept in that mailbox for as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the correspondence.

Email is delivered through Zoho Mail, which processes the message in order to transmit it.

Analytics

Visits are measured with Plausible Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not collect or store personal information. It records aggregate figures such as page views, referring site, country, and broad device type. Individual visitors cannot be identified or followed between sites.

Data stored in your browser

Two preferences are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them on your next visit:

  • web-theme, whether you chose light or dark mode
  • web-locale, the language you selected

These stay on your device, are never sent to a server, and are removed if you clear your browser's site data.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard request information such as IP address in order to serve pages and protect the service.

The component library

The @eagami/ui package collects nothing. It makes no network requests, sets no cookies, and contains no analytics. Installing or using it sends no data to eagami.

Your choices

To ask what correspondence is held about you, or to have it deleted, email info@eagami.com. Analytics data is aggregate and cannot be traced back to an individual, so it cannot be retrieved or deleted per person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the site changelog.
