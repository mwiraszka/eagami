Eagami does not advertise or track across other sites. There are no accounts, and the only personal information the site receives is what you type into the contact form.

What the site measures

Visits are measured with Plausible Analytics, which sets no cookies and collects no personal information. It records aggregate figures such as page views, referring site, country, and broad device type. Individual visitors cannot be identified or followed between sites.

Two preferences are saved in your browser's local storage:

  • web-theme, whether you chose light or dark mode
  • web-locale, the language you selected

These stay on your device, are never sent to a server, and disappear if you clear your browser's site data.

The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard request information such as IP address in order to serve pages and protect the service.

When you get in touch

Sending a message through the contact form emails your name, email address, and the message itself to eagami. Nothing is written to a database and nothing is used for marketing. Messages are kept for as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the correspondence.

The component library

The @eagami/ui package collects nothing. It makes no network requests, sets no cookies, and contains no analytics. Installing or using it sends no data to eagami.

Your choices

To ask what correspondence is held about you, or to have it deleted, email privacy@eagami.com. Analytics data is aggregate and cannot be traced back to an individual, so it cannot be retrieved or deleted per person.

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes are noted in the site changelog.
